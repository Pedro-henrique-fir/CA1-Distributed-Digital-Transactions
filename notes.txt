In a normal hotel booking, the customer depends on other parties for almost everything. The hotel keeps the booking records and decides about refunds, and the payment usually goes through a booking website or a bank before it reaches the hotel. So the customer cannot really check what is happening and has to trust everyone in the middle. My idea is to use the blockchain as the middle layer between the hotel and the guest. In simple terms, the smart contract keeps the records, holds the money and follows the rules automatically.

Trust and transparency: every booking is saved on the blockchain, so the guest and the hotel can both check it at any time.

Refunds: if the guest cancels before the deadline, the contract sends the refund automatically, without waiting for the hotel to decide.

Payment intermediaries: the money stays in the contract, like an escrow and go to the hotel only when the check in is confirmed. No bank or booking website needs to hold it.


Use Cases

Hotel: register rooms, set the room price, view reservations and receive payments

Guest: check available rooms, book a room and pay, view a reservation, cancel a booking before the deadline and get the money back when eligible

Rules:

Only the hotel owner can add rooms and set their prices
Each booking is for one room and one night
Two guests cannot book the same room for the same date
The guest must pay the exact price to confirm a booking
The payment stays in the smart contract until it is refunded or released to the hotel
Only the guest who made the booking can cancel it
If the guest cancels at least 24 hours before check-in, they receive a full refund, but after this deadline cancellation is not allowed
Once a booking is confirmed, its price and details cannot be changed
The hotel can withdraw the payment after the check-in time
Each booking has a status like confirmed, cancelled or completed
The same payment can not be refunded or withdrawn more than once




Data

For the rooms the smart contract need to store the room number, the price per night and whether the room is available or not.
For each booking, it need to keep a booking id, the room number, the guest wallet address, the check-in date, the amount paid and the booking status.

The contract also needs to know which rooms are already booked for each date to avoid double bookings.

For personal information like names and emails, I would keep this in the hotel private system instead of putting it on the blockchain. The idea is to connect both systems using the booking id, so the hotel can identify the guest when they arrive.

Integration with the Hotel System

When a guest makes a booking, the smart contract checks the availability, receives the payment and records the reservation on the blockchain.

After the transaction is confirmed, the hotel system can read the booking information and link it to the guest personal details stored privately.

This way, the blockchain handles the booking rules, payments and refunds, while the hotel still manages guest information and the check-in process.
